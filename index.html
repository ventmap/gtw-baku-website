FAQ

When is GTW Baku 2027?

GTW Baku takes place in April 2027 in Baku, Azerbaijan. Exact dates will be announced soon.

Who is GTW Baku for?

Founders, investors, companies and institutions that want to connect Azerbaijan with the US market and the wider GTW network.

How can my company partner?

Email Valid and we will shape a role around your goals.

Is GTW Baku part of a bigger network?

Yes. GTW Baku is part of Global Tech Weekend (GTW), a US-born event series. In 2027, Global Tech Weekend runs editions in Baku, Tashkent, Tbilisi, Astana and Los Angeles.
