A new market for the GTW platform

One city. Many hosts. A global network.

GTW Baku will be a decentralized technology and innovation platform built across the city with local companies, institutions, universities, hubs and community leaders.

Rather than placing the entire ecosystem inside one venue, GTW enables trusted local hosts to create focused events for their communities while connecting every gathering through one international platform.

GTW Baku is not being built as another conference. It is being built as infrastructure for relationships, visibility and cross-border opportunity.
