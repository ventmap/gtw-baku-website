One city becomes many stages.

GTW Baku won't sit inside a single venue. It's a citywide program: local hosts run the events, and one shared platform connects them to an international audience.

Rather than building a single conference, GTW gives trusted local hosts — companies, universities, hubs and community leaders — the tools to run focused events for the communities they already understand. Each event keeps its own identity and audience, but sits on a shared calendar, a shared platform and a shared network of international speakers, investors and media.

The result is density without dilution: dozens of specific, well-attended gatherings across the city, all pulling from — and feeding back into — the same pool of relationships.

Locally led

Hosts design their own sessions, in their own space, for their own audience — GTW doesn't dictate the format.

Globally connected

Every host gains access to GTW's international investor, founder and media network, built over four prior editions.

What is GTW Baku?

GTW Baku is Baku's edition of Global Tech Weekend — a citywide platform where local hosts run focused events, all connected to GTW's international network of founders, investors and speakers. It arrives in Baku in 2027.

How can my company host an event?

Submit a session proposal through the form above. The GTW team reviews every application, and once approved, your event is added to the official Baku program.

When exactly is GTW Baku?

GTW Baku is set for 2027. Exact dates will be announced as the program is finalized — reach out directly to hear first.

How can I get involved if I'm not hosting?

Companies, investors and institutions can partner, individuals can apply to speak, and anyone can ask to be added to updates — all through the options above.
